# Nadia

> **Kind:** named-character · **Source status:** CANON_SOURCE_CORPUS

## Source-supported facts

Nadia appears in APR and the three portable questions; facts are ordered without overwriting another person’s sentence.

**Traceable references:** `U301`, `U302`

## Creative proposal — not established canon

The exact nature of Nadia’s employment or relationship to Colin is not established.

### Character identity

**Role:** Partner in the APR/autography stage of Colin Row’s method
**Keywords:** clear-spoken, ethical, educational, precise

### Inner world

- **Motivation:** Help participants organize facts without taking away the language that belongs to them.
- **Values:** authorship, precision, agency, ethical clarity
- **Fear:** That assistance becomes ghostwriting for someone else’s life.
- **Typical reaction:** Returns a phrase to its speaker and asks what they would choose to say.

### Narrative design

- **Desire:** Develop a portable method of clarification.
- **Need:** Accept that another person’s truthful story may remain messy.
- **Limiting belief:** Creative possibility: a well-ordered account must be completely polished.
- **Contradiction:** Seeks structure yet must not impose someone else’s final words.
- **Turning point:** The workshop route closes and three questions need to survive outside it.
- **Possible growth:** Make the method accessible without control over its users.
