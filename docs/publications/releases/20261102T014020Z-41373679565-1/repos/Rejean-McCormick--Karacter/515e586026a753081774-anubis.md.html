## Source-supported summary

Anubis examines the scales of passage and comes to see balance as a living process capable of being remade.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Guide passage with responsibility and fairness.

**Fear:** Losing the integrity of the scales.

**Narrative contradiction:** An impartial system can overlook a singular life.

**Possible growth:** Move from rigid verdicts to thoughtful rebalancing.
