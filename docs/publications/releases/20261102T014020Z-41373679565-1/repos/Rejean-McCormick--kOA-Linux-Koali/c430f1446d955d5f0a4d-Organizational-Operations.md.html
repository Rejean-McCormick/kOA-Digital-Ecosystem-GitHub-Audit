# Organizational Operations

> **Authority and status:** This wiki is an explanatory navigation layer. The repository contracts, accepted ADRs, normative documents, and executable validators remain authoritative for kOA-Linux Operating System. Public initkoa.org pages explain the wider kOA ecosystem and the product capabilities provided by its independently documented subsystems.

## Intended environment

An organization uses Orgo to replace fragile inbox-, chat- and memory-based coordination with durable cases, tasks, functional ownership, response windows, escalation and closure.

## Typical workflows

- service requests and incident handling;
- approvals and reviews;
- procurement or maintenance;
- compliance and audit follow-up;
- HR or sensitive case management;
- project execution and recurring operational cycles;
- role inboxes that convert communications into accountable work.

## Why kOA-Linux matters

The operating system isolates the organization's data, applies identity and resource boundaries, supports offline nodes, provides backups and controls external publication or AI access.
