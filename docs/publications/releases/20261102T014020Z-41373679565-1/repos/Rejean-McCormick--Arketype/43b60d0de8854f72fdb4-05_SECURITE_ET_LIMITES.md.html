# Security, privacy and limits

Arketype ships neutral templates and **no actual testimony, personnel records, clinical information, security credentials or domain facts**.

- An empty template is not an authenticated collection system. Real witness or employee data requires explicit access controls, retention rules, lawful authority, audit mechanisms, and protections appropriate to its context.
- A linked external state does not automatically grant permission to read it. Hosted projections and Context Packs require their own access checks.
- Scientific, operational, legal and factual validation is **not** provided by a successful `check` or `build-v9`.
- Publication and activation are distinct, authorized lifecycle steps; Arketype performs neither.
- Machine-assisted transcripts, interpretations and summaries remain derived representations unless separately accepted under an applicable contract.
- Content from a private source must not be copied into a public catalogue or generated view by default.

The library is a framework-neutral design aid. The responsible domain owner must qualify any deployed system independently.
