# Coordinates & Applicability

> **v9 compatibility note:** This page documents the retained v6 portable-state semantics. V9 preserves the contract as a frozen substrate and may wrap/project it through Logical Artifacts without redefining it. See [[Version-Stack-v6-v7-v8-v9]].

Kristal v6 distinguishes **where an assertion sits in the domain** from **where the assertion applies**.

## Coordinates

`coordinates` are the domain geometry of an assertion.

Examples:

- source/target biological layer;
- source/target scale;
- recipe tradition or variant family;
- vehicle operating condition;
- technological axis;
- taxonomic rank;
- observation condition;
- process phase.

Coordinates can form a hierarchy, matrix, graph, multiplex, causal network or other geometry. They are not necessarily “minor qualifiers”.

## Applicability

`applicability` is the envelope in which the assertion should be interpreted as applicable.

Typical dimensions include:

- domain / subdomain;
- jurisdiction;
- time window;
- environment;
- language;
- tenant/context.

Examples:

- a Quebec administrative rule in a specified effective period;
- a diagnostic relation for one model year and powertrain;
- a maintenance rule before freezing conditions;
- a botanical occurrence in a territory and time interval.

## Same concept, different role

Time can be a coordinate or applicability constraint.

A historical occurrence date describes the content. A regulation effective period describes when the rule applies.

Do not collapse these just because both are temporal.
