# Architect

Architect is an **articulation/planning/rendering capability**. It transforms eligible knowledge and operating context into plans or user-facing representations without inventing new factual claims.

## Kristal v6 input rule

Architect is no longer defined as consuming “validated canonical truth only”. Eligibility is governed by the active **Reader Policy**.

A Reader Policy may permit, for example:

- reference-only material;
- validated material;
- high-valuation material;
- research views containing Working, disputed or low-valuation material;
- creative/symbolic corpora with their classifications intact.

Architect must preserve visible epistemic distinctions such as source artifact status, assertion status, valuation, validation status, `validated_as`, authority channel/scope and disagreement state.

## Boundary

Architect may summarize, plan, render and propose. It must not create authority recognition or silently convert a hypothesis/disputed claim into a fact. New knowledge claims return through an appropriate governed knowledge path.
