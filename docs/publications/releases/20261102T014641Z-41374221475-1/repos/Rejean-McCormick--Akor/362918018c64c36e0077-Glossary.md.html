# Glossary

**Akor** — proposed bridge for inter-system integration **and, more importantly, meaningful interaction between children** during shared learning missions.

**Accord / learning in concert** — a relationship where distinct contributions work toward a shared, educationally useful outcome.

**Kontexte** — owner of reusable situations, role and mission definitions, localized overlays and narrative intentions.

**kompose** — deterministic coordination design, previews, rule validation and mock replay; not the canonical live owner of a school mission.

**Orgo** — canonical operational engine for Cases, Tasks, Signals, WorkflowVersions, transitions and permission-governed runtime actions.

**Orgo_Worlds** — owner of Worlds, WorldReleases, world memberships and contextual routing—not live Orgo task state.

**Ekole** — teacher/student experience, curricular content/publications and pedagogical evidence.

**Narrative role** — a fictional position such as baker or farmer; not an authenticated authorization role.

**Role projection** — the information and allowed actions that a recipient may see after server-side authorization.

**Fragment** — an item of potentially role-restricted scenario information that should not be revealed until authorized.

**Join / checkpoint** — a condition requiring contributions or information to be combined before progress; a source definition alone is not runtime enforcement.

**WorldRelease** — an immutable release context referenced by a running mission, owned by Orgo_Worlds.

**Operational evidence** — a record that the work system reached a state; not proof of a curriculum objective.

**Learning evidence** — learner work or reasoning, associated with an objective and support context, reviewed within Ekole.

**Synthetic / preview-only** — fictional test content that is safe to inspect but not approved for deployment with real learners.

**Idempotency** — repeated processing of an equivalent operation does not duplicate a business effect.

**Fallback** — an explicitly designed and authorized alternative when a partner, service or resource is unavailable.
