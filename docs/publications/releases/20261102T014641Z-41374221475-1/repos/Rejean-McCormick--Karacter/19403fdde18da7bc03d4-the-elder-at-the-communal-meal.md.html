> **Kind:** unnamed-role-character · **Source status:** SOURCE_EXPLICIT_NON_GOVERNING

## Source-supported facts

An elder corrects King Klown at a meal, and he accepts this; the scene is explicit in the L’Asile source branch, not governing canon.

- **Values:** hospitality, truthfulness, mutual respect
- **Fear:** Creative possibility: admiration for an outsider will silence local expertise.

- **Contradiction:** Offers hospitality while refusing deferential silence.
- **Turning point:** King Klown openly accepts her correction.
- **Possible growth:** See disagreement become part of respectful shared culture.
