# Curriculum

## Overview

The current pack contains **32 draft courses**, organized in **two independent learning branches** and **seven pathways**. The teaching materials are in Canadian French (`fr-CA`); the titles below are English descriptions for navigation, not replacements for the canonical course names.

- **Message and Living Tradition:** 20 courses, **62 planned hours**.
- **Miracles and Mysteries of Faith:** 12 courses, **37 planned hours**.
- **Total:** **99 planned hours** across all units, not a credit-bearing qualification.

Each unit normally has four learning sessions: **texts and context**, **tradition and distinctions**, **pastoral question**, and **workshop/application**. Units include an observable deliverable and teacher evaluation criteria. The duration figures are design estimates, not evidence of completed or approved teaching.

## Learning pathways

The seven `pack.json` pathways are sequences of selected courses; a pathway is **not** the same thing as a complete third branch.

| Pathway ID | English description | Courses |

| `EPC-SAINTS` | Saints: Virtues in Action | 4 |
| `EPC-SOCIAL` | Social Engagement and Pastoral Practice | 6 |
| `EPC-FOI-APPRO` | Deepening Faith While Facing Questions | 5 |
| `EPC-DECOUVERTE` | Open Introduction: Two Distinct Entry Points | 5 |

The introductory pathway can present options from both branches without turning either into a mandatory doctrinal gate for the other.

## Updating the curriculum

Change a course's canonical Kristal, rebuild its pinned state, run the projector, and validate its prerequisites. Never edit the generated `pack.json` as if it were the source of truth. See [Authoring and Projection](Authoring-and-Projection.md).

## Editorial status

These are **detailed learning outlines**, not 99 hours of finished, independently reviewed lesson content. Assessment, historical attribution, theology, rights, and safeguarding all require completion before publication. See [Governance and Safeguarding](Governance-and-Safeguarding.md).
