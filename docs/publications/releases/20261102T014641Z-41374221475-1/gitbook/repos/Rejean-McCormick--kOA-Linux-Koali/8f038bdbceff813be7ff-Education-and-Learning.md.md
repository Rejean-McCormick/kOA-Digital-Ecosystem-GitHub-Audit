# Education and Learning

> **Authority and status:** This wiki is an explanatory navigation layer. The repository contracts, accepted ADRs, normative documents, and executable validators remain authoritative for kOA-Linux Operating System. Public initkoa.org pages explain the wider kOA ecosystem and the product capabilities provided by its independently documented subsystems.

## Intended environment

Konnaxion can distribute learning materials, learning paths, mentorship and community knowledge. Kristals preserve provenance and offline usability. Semantik Architect can render structured material into multiple languages. Orgo can coordinate educational cases, tasks, groups and support workflows.

## Example capabilities

- offline learning packages for low-connectivity environments;
- multilingual content and localized navigation;
- mentorship requests and learning groups;
- portable competence or certification evidence;
- routing support needs to accountable roles;
- preservation of learning resources and provenance.

## Important boundary

Educational validity, certification authority and curriculum recognition require explicit authority channels. A generated summary or automated completion signal does not create recognized competence by itself.
