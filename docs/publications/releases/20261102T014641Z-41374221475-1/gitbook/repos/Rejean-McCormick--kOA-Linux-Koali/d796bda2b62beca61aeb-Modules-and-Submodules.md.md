# Modules and Submodules

> **Reading model:** The main path explains what the kOA ecosystem enables. The technical reference explains how kOA-Linux hosts, isolates, verifies, and operates those capabilities. Product-specific details remain in each subsystem's own documentation.

## Navigation principle

This Wiki names the major systems so readers can understand the full operating cycle. It does not duplicate their internal documentation. Follow the linked product Wikis and repositories for detailed models, routes, schemas, services, and user workflows.

## Runtime, governance, and storage

- **kOA-Linux Operating System** — sovereign local runtime, profiles, isolation, trust, resources, lifecycle, offline continuity, backup, and recovery.
- **Governance Policy Runtime** — policy evaluation for authorization, disclosure, consent, exceptions, and governed transitions.
- **kOA Mediatheque** — local, private-by-default authority for files and media held by the installation.
- **UCKK** — external Moodle platform for online learning and dissemination; exchange is explicit and does not make UCKK a local subsystem.

## External and symbolic layers

Additional tools, narrative systems, and partner platforms may connect to the ecosystem, but they do not silently become authority. The symbolic layer helps make the architecture memorable and transmissible; it does not determine rights, decisions, or host privilege.
