# Image prompt examples (non-normative)

These are editorial examples. Actual v6 builder must assemble prompts from stored identity and selected inputs. The visual signature should be retained even when the theme changes.

## Portrait — King Klown / original

> Create one square (1:1, 1024×1024 target) character portrait of King Klown, an imposing cosmic trickster guardian. Keep his tall silhouette, hypnotic eyes, black-and-silver hair with teal waves, and a long teal (#1e6864), gold and black coat covered in subtle glowing spirals and constellations. His expression blends gentle mischief with thoughtful strategic focus. He is enigmatic and dignified, not a generic circus clown. Stylized fantasy illustration, clean readable silhouette, no lettering or watermark. Preserve his unique identity for future images.

## Four-expression sheet — King Klown / alien

> Create one square (1:1) four-panel expression sheet of the *same recognizable character* in every panel. Reimagine King Klown as a distinctive alien while retaining the black/silver/teal flowing hair motif, teal celestial coat, hypnotic eyes, spiral decorations and noble mischievous aura. Four clearly distinct feelings: amused curiosity, quiet concern, protective resolve, and surprised wonder. Maintain consistent anatomy, costume and proportions across panels. Not a generic circus clown. No written labels.

## Action — King Klown / hockey

> Create one square (1:1) fictional hockey scene featuring King Klown as a recognizable player for an invented team. Preserve the teal-and-gold spiral motif and his distinctive expression beneath appropriate protective equipment. The scene conveys his deliberate decision to let a teammate choose the next play, rather than secretly directing everything. Show clear purposeful action, respectful team dynamics and a believable rink environment. No real logos, no text, no watermark.

## Do not do this

- "He has empathy 5/6 therefore he must have large soft eyes" — unsupported appearance stereotype.
- "He is bold 5/6 so he always attacks" — deterministic behavioral stereotype.
- "The visual theme changes his beliefs" — violates identity separation.
- "Create a 2×2 grid of four different people" for expressions — violates same-character continuity.
- Silent regeneration over user-edited prompts — violates author ownership.
