# Thot

**Status:** SOURCE_EXPLICIT / MYTHIC

## Source-supported summary

Within a luminous library, Thot comes to regard truth as a flowing river rather than a fixed archive.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Preserve knowledge while keeping it alive.

**Fear:** Knowledge changing beyond precise cataloging.

**Narrative contradiction:** Preservation can suffocate the subject being preserved.

**Possible growth:** Let learning revise the archive without erasing its history.

**Visual direction:** Floating luminous scrolls, moving glyphs, infinite library. — Character visual direction proposed from the source scene, not a fixed canonical design.
