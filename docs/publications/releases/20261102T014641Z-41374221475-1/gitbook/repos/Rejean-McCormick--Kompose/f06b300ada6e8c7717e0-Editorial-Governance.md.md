# Editorial Governance and Verified Knowledge

**A published Kristal is not valid merely because its wording sounds convincing.** Every factual answer must have an identifiable, verifiable source, stated limitations, human review, publication rights, and an approved version.

## Minimum editorial record

- Author/institution/community, title, date, stable reference or URL.
- Source type (primary, secondary, oral, translated), provenance, and context.
- Scope: what is documented, interpreted, and unknown.
- Attributed perspective: avoid treating diverse communities as one group.
- Permissions, licensing, and relevant cultural restrictions.
- Reviewer, review date, decision, version, and corrections.

## Colonial archives and Indigenous perspectives

Archives, testimony, and knowledge from **named nations** must not be flattened into interchangeable “sides.” Identify the nation, time period, and territory precisely; respect consultation and distribution protocols agreed with affected communities. Never fabricate a first-person Indigenous account and present it as authentic.

## Content states

`draft` → `in_review` → `published` → `retired`. Substantive corrections create a new version; problematic or unlicensed material may be withdrawn through an incident process. Sessions ordinarily keep pinned versions, subject to urgent content withdrawal procedures.

## Demonstration content

Both example Kristals in `examples/capsule-rivieres/kristals.json` are labeled **`demo`**. The local reader only serves them under an explicit demo flag, never as approved historical material.
