# The Koali usage palette

The palette is the primary public-facing taxonomy. It is intentionally smaller than the full internal capability model.

## 1. Find & Understand
Find information, prior work, sources, context, expertise, relationships, and history. Investigate signals, compare claims, and build a usable picture of a situation.

## 2. Learn & Share
Teach, learn, mentor, onboard, document practice, preserve know-how, connect learning with evidence, and make knowledge transferable.

## 3. Collaborate & Create
Research together, form teams, design, write, prototype, build projects, develop policy, and keep the reasons behind creative choices connected.

## 4. Choose & Govern
Evaluate options, deliberate across perspectives, prioritize, allocate resources, decide, document dissent, and preserve why a choice was made.

## 5. Organize & Act
Turn goals, decisions, requests, and mandates into responsibilities, tasks, dependencies, schedules, verification, escalation, and closure.

## 6. Respond & Coordinate
Handle incidents, disruptions, crises, fast-changing field conditions, and operational exceptions while maintaining a shared situation.

## 7. Remember & Improve
Run postmortems, compare outcomes over time, preserve institutional capability, detect recurring patterns, verify whether interventions worked, and improve future decisions.

## 8. Disseminate & Connect to the Public
Turn validated knowledge, research, decisions, and experience into courses, guides, media, public explanations, libraries, and channels without severing provenance.

## Why a palette instead of a feature list

A real Koali use usually combines several colors. A cyber incident may combine understand + collaborate + respond + act + remember. A public research channel may combine understand + learn + disseminate + remember. The combination is the use case.
