# Message hierarchy

## Level 1 — memorable line
> **From knowledge to coordinated action.**

## Level 2 — product explanation
> **Koali connects knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory.**

## Level 3 — problem explanation
Organizations lose context when work moves between documents, conversations, decisions, task systems, learning systems, and archives. Koali is designed to preserve continuity across those transitions.

## Level 4 — versatility explanation
The same continuity problem appears in maintenance, research, education, management, public services, culture, field work, and crises. The sector changes; the recurring pattern remains.

## Level 5 — architecture explanation
Kristal preserves semantic knowledge and memory. Konnaxion connects people around it. Orgo turns decisions and needs into organized work. UCKK can turn knowledge into learning and multimedia dissemination.

## Level 6 — differentiators
Multilingual · offline/local · AI-optional · provenance-aware · role-aware · suitable for sensitive-data architectures where documented.

## Level 7 — evidence
Use the 120-scenario Atlas, canonical references, and runtime validation status to distinguish possibility from proof.
