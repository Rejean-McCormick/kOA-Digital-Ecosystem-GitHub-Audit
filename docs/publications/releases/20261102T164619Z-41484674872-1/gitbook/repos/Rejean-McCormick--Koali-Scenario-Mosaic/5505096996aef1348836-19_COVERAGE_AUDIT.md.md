# Coverage audit

The Atlas is intentionally balanced across eight primary categories: 15 scenarios each, for 120 total.

## Coverage objectives

The corpus should demonstrate:

- individual and team work;
- organizational and inter-organizational work;
- public institutions and community settings;
- knowledge-intensive and operational workflows;
- routine work and exceptional incidents;
- learning and public dissemination;
- field/offline and connected environments;
- multilingual and local-first value;
- sensitive-data and high-consequence boundaries;
- both mundane and high-impact situations.

## Biases to monitor

Avoid over-representing crisis scenarios, corporate knowledge work, or technically sophisticated sectors. The strongest proof of generality comes from showing the same patterns in ordinary work, craft, education, maintenance, research, public service, culture, and crisis.

## v2 structural correction

The addition of 24 pattern families is critical: breadth should be understood as **recombination of recurring patterns**, not as a collection of unrelated vertical features.
