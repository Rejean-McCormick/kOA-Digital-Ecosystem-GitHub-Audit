# Roadmap

The roadmap favors a **single vertical mission** before broad platform integration. These are proposed milestones, not releases already shipped.

## Phase 0 — Establish Akor's boundaries

- Create the `akor` repository with this wiki and a versioned integration contract register.
- Pin tested versions/hashes of all six source repositories.
- Decide deployment shape (server-side adapter, sidecar or service), threat model and rollback.
- Re-run dry-run validation from the existing prequalification bridge.

**Exit:** owner matrix approved; read-only review reproducible; no writes to production.

## Phase 1 — Reviewed solo mission

- Resolve the `qc-pri-ma-006` vs `qc-pri-ma-008` mapping with pedagogical review; preserve the original source.
- Produce a versioned, approved bakery variant and WorldRelease in a test world.
- Qualify Orgo workflow for a **solo** journey and its authorized actions.
- Implement Akor server-side session mapping, learner-safe projection and teacher start/pause/close.
- Connect an Ekole learner screen and store the appropriate learning artifact separately.
- Exercise revisions, replay, outages and teacher interruption using synthetic identities.

**Exit:** a fictional child account can finish a persistent solo mission through real Orgo state, with full technical evidence and no privacy leaks.

## Phase 2 — Genuine interaction between children

- Add pair/group membership and role-specific access decisions.
- Implement Orgo-enforced fragment release and join checkpoints.
- Support classroom communication/hand-offs with age-appropriate moderation or teacher facilitation.
- Handle absent partners, slow participants, reconnects, reassignment, duplicates and concurrent updates.
- Provide meaningful roles for every child and avoid contribution/popularity rankings.

**Exit:** two or more fictional learner sessions must **need one another's contributions** to advance, without exposing private material or deadlocking.

## Phase 3 — Teacher learning evidence and curriculum review

- Link individual work and supports used to Ekole objectives and teacher review.
- Make operational success and academic achievement visibly distinct.
- Add traceable provenance, audit and revocation, along with data retention controls.
- Extend mission designs only after review of each subject and difficulty level.

**Exit:** an authorized teacher can explain *who demonstrated what, with what help*, without overclaiming official curricular coverage.

## Phase 4 — School pilot readiness

- Security, privacy, incident response, consent, accessibility and retention review.
- Usability sessions with educators and suitable child safeguards.
- Backup/restore, operational monitoring, rollbacks and degradation testing.
- Controlled deployment plan and teacher training.

**Exit:** institutional sign-off and full risk acceptance—not merely a passing simulator.

## Out of scope for initial Akor

A new public social platform, unmoderated chat, automated grading or diagnosis, duplicating Orgo's workflow engine, replacing Ekole, or automatically publishing Kontexte drafts.
