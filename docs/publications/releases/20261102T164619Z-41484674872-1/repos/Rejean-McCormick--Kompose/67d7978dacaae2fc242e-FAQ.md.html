# Frequently Asked Questions

### Does KOMPOSE replace KOR or Orgo?

**Neither.** KOR is an optional presentation client (ADR 0006). **Orgo is the sole operational authority for real Tandem Case/Task workflows, transitions, dispatch, and permissions** (ADR 0008). KOMPOSE only validates, designs and simulates those workflows locally; it does not grant real access.

### Do students need USB drives?

No. The concept uses a **browser-based Entry Door** and virtual Kristals, which fit managed school Chromebooks better.

### Are exactly 20 roles required?

No. The demo has 20 roles; the design allows configurable quantities and capacities.

### Does KOMPOSE make final role assignments?

No. A future production solver should propose a compatible plan, which a teacher approves and may revise. The complete solver **has not been developed yet**.

### Does the top third always choose first?

No. It is an **optional** policy. If the diagnostic has not been shown to support fair comparison, the session must use a non-ranking mode.

### Can a Kristal answer any question?

No. It serves prepared questions matched to identifiers and sourced responses. It does not generate new historical facts.

### Can a guardian permanently withhold knowledge?

No. Mediation, time limits, transfers, and fallback access must prevent deadlock and exclusion.

### Can teachers record affinities and aversions?

A future teacher console may hold minimized, private, temporary collaboration signals solely to help maintain a safe learning environment. They are not social evaluations of students.

### Does the local P1 preview verifier authorize a WorldRelease or real Task?

**No.** The strict synthetic JSON verifier returns `runtime_admitted: false`, `permissions_verified: false`, and `world_release_verified: false`. The source fixture has 11 synthetic tasks; none is an Orgo operation. See [[Ekole-P1-Preview]].

### Can B6 run an actual Kontexte bakery mission?

**No.** It compiles a fictional, non-executable plan of three roles and three human-review steps, supports restricted projections and checks synthetic Orgo receipts. A replayed fixture proves no teacher identity, fragment delivery or task completion in Orgo. See [[Kontexte-B6-Preview]].

### Is it ready for use at school?

**No.** The repository is a local functional reference with schemas and tests. Real authentication, permissions, the allocation solver, verified content, compliance review, and accessible interfaces remain necessary.

### Are the historical documents already verified?

No. All example documents are explicitly **fictional** and must never be represented as authentic archives.
