# Legba

**Role:** Guardian of thresholds
**Status:** SOURCE_EXPLICIT / MYTHIC
**Source:** `CORPUS/mythos-gods/Legba le premier pas du voyageur 15709637d3f7806e98f7eb28cccf0075.md`

## Source-supported summary

Legba holds open the crossing paths of others and is challenged to walk through one of those doors himself.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Keep pathways and meaningful choices open.

**Fear:** Abandoning those who rely on him at the threshold.

**Narrative contradiction:** Guiding journeys may keep him from beginning his own.

**Possible growth:** Take one step beyond the doorway he tends.

**Visual direction:** Luminous crossroads, staff and a small silver key. — Character visual direction proposed from the source scene, not a fixed canonical design.
