## Source-supported summary

On a battlefield of ash, Morrigan questions whether what ends can also prepare the ground for what comes next.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** End what can no longer serve life.

**Fear:** Losing her identity if destruction is not the only task.

**Narrative contradiction:** Her endings may contain the seed of creation.

**Possible growth:** Treat the aftermath of destruction as her responsibility too.

**Visual direction:** Ashen battlefield, a dormant seed among broken steel. — Character visual direction proposed from the source scene, not a fixed canonical design.
