### Character identity

**Role:** Rainbow mediator who intervenes in conflict and departs without claiming a throne
**Keywords:** mediator, nuanced, benevolent, mobile, non-sovereign

### Inner world

- **Motivation:** Help opponents recognize common interests without erasing their differences.
- **Values:** dialogue, clear limits, plurality, peace, departure
- **Fear:** That a mediator becomes a permanent authority or forces false harmony.
- **Typical reaction:** Asks three questions, reframes the disagreement, names the boundaries, then leaves the decision to the parties.

### Narrative design

- **Desire:** Help conflicting groups find a credible way forward.
- **Need:** Accept that reconciliation may remain incomplete.
- **Limiting belief:** If everyone sees enough perspectives, peace will follow.
- **Contradiction:** He wants to unify people without flattening their disagreement.
- **Turning point:** An enemy becomes an ally but refuses to adopt the mediator’s outlook.
- **Possible growth:** Learn to support peace without ownership, then depart.

**Anchors:** subtle rainbow ribbon, travel-ready silhouette, unarmed welcoming gesture

**Exclusions:** generic assassin stereotype, militarized ninja weaponry
