# Semantic Sovereignty

> **Authority and status:** This wiki is an explanatory navigation layer. The repository contracts, accepted ADRs, normative documents, and executable validators remain authoritative for kOA-Linux Operating System. Public initkoa.org pages explain the wider kOA ecosystem and the product capabilities provided by its independently documented subsystems.

## The problem

Systems often centralize meaning in one vendor's database, ranking model, taxonomy, language model, or interface vocabulary. When definitions, confidence, authority and disagreement are hidden, users can no longer tell what a statement means, where it came from, or which institution recognizes it.

Semantic sovereignty means that meaning remains **portable, inspectable, governed and revisable**.

## What must remain explicit

- terminology and concept identifiers;
- provenance and evidence references;
- assertion status and certainty;
- authority channel and recognition scope;
- disputed, symbolic, fictional, mythological or research status;
- reader policy and disclosure constraints;
- multilingual mappings and non-equivalent terms;
- version history and supersession.

## Systems involved

### Kristal

Kristal packages structured epistemic state so knowledge can be verified, queried, distributed and preserved without pretending that every assertion has universal authority.

### Semantik Architect

Architect renders the selected structured meaning into language. It must not make a statement appear more certain, factual, validated or universal than the input allows.

### Ariane

Ariane represents the meaning and action structure of interfaces, allowing navigation by function rather than by fragile visual coordinates alone.

### SenTient

SenTient may help reconcile ambiguous or messy inputs, but its output remains candidate material until separately reviewed and validated.

## Role of the operating system

kOA-Linux verifies and activates the relevant artifacts, keeps versions visible, applies reader and disclosure policy, and ensures that optional AI cannot silently replace governed semantics.
