# kOA-Linux Operating System

> **Reading model:** The main path explains what the kOA ecosystem enables. The technical reference explains how kOA-Linux hosts, isolates, verifies, and operates those capabilities. Product-specific details remain in each subsystem's own documentation.

> **kOA-Linux Operating System is the sovereign runtime for a wider knowledge-to-action ecosystem.**

## The big picture

Communities, schools, organizations, researchers, public institutions, and cultural networks often possess information, experience, and motivation but lack a reliable path from what they know to what they can do together.

The wider **kOA ecosystem** is designed to operate that path:

```text
Know → Choose → Act → Remember → Know better
```

In operational terms:

```text
signals, documents, experience, and cultural memory
→ structured and verifiable knowledge
→ learning, consultation, and deliberation
→ legitimate decisions with visible reasoning
→ roles, tasks, coordination, escalation, and closure
→ evidence, institutional memory, and renewed knowledge
```

The aim is not to build one giant application. kOA is a **system of systems**: each platform owns a distinct part of the cycle, while kOA-Linux provides local execution, isolation, offline continuity, trust, policy, resources, recovery, and controlled integration.

## What the ecosystem does

| Collective need | kOA capability |
| --- | --- |
| Turn fragmented information into reusable knowledge | Semantic processing, [Kristal artifacts](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/kristal-framework), provenance, versions, uncertainty, and verification |
| Help people learn and build competence | [Konnaxion](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Konnaxion/wiki) learning modules, portable resources, multilingual rendering, and offline packages |
| Make disagreement understandable | Structured consultation, argumentation, drafting, and visible decision procedures |
| Improve judgment without replacing democracy | EkoH credibility signals and Smart Vote advisory readings kept separate from sovereign results |
| Turn decisions into accountable action | [Orgo](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Orgo/wiki) Cases, Tasks, roles, routing, deadlines, escalation, closure, and operational memory |
| Help groups become durable organizations | Membership, purpose, governance, roles, workflows, responsibility, and review loops |
| Preserve culture with authority and context | Kreative and Konservation: provenance, consent, rights, community authority, exhibitions, and cultural memory |
| Work across languages and meanings | [SemantiK Architect](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/SemantiK_Architect), governed terminology, multilingual mappings, and semantic sovereignty |
| Reconcile unstructured information with semantic systems | [SenTient](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/SenTient) extraction, entity linking, structured review, and candidate knowledge |
| Navigate complex software by intention | [Ariane](Ariane) semantic interface graphs and assisted navigation |
| Preserve and exchange learning media | Local kOA media authority plus the operational [UCKK Mediatheque](https://uckk.org), under separate databases and authorities |
| Continue locally and offline | kOA-Linux profiles, local storage, verified artifacts, queued exchange, backup, and safe degradation |
| Deploy Konnaxion as a governed local service | [Konnaxion Capsule Manager](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Konnaxion_Capsule_Manager) packaging, verification, deployment, backup, restore, and rollback |
| Keep AI subordinate to governed workflows | AI may assist extraction, translation, synthesis, or routing; it does not create invisible authority |

## Modules and systems

### Public knowledge and civic collaboration — Konnaxion

[Konnaxion](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Konnaxion) is the commons-facing platform. Its modules cover learning, governance, innovation, culture, and collective intelligence:

- **KonnectED** — Knowledge, CertifiKation
- **Ethikos** — Korum, Konsultations
- **keenKonnect** — Konstruct, Stockage
- **Kreative** — Konservation, Kontact
- **Kollective Intelligence** — EkoH, Smart Vote

Use the [Konnaxion Wiki](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Konnaxion/wiki) for detailed module and submodule documentation.

For packaging and governed local deployment, use the [Konnaxion Capsule Manager repository](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Konnaxion_Capsule_Manager).

### Organizational execution — Orgo

[Orgo](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Orgo) turns signals and decisions into Cases, Tasks, roles, routing, reactivity windows, escalation, closure, and recurring review.

Use the [Orgo Wiki](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Orgo/wiki) for its complete organizational and operational model.

### Knowledge, semantics, and language

- **[Kristal Framework](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/kristal-framework)** — portable, status-aware knowledge artifacts with provenance, validation, versioning, and offline runtime packs.
- **[SenTient](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/SenTient)** — optional semantic reconciliation and structured-extraction workbench whose outputs remain candidates until accepted.
- **[SemantiK Architect](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/SemantiK_Architect)** — deterministic multilingual realization of structured meaning.
- **[Ariane](Ariane)** — semantic navigation and interaction orchestration inside the kOA environment.

See [Related Wikis and Documentation](Related-Wikis) for the public repositories and product-specific documentation. This Wiki presents their place in the ecosystem without duplicating their complete specifications.

### Media, learning resources, and provenance

The **kOA Mediatheque** provides local, private-by-default, offline-capable media authority. The **UCKK Mediatheque** provides the online learning and publication environment available at [uckk.org](https://uckk.org).

The UCKK Mediatheque is operational, but its source repository is not public at this time. The local and online Mediatheques use compatible descriptive frames while retaining separate databases, identities, authorities, and lifecycles.

See [UCKK Mediatheque and exchange boundary](UCKK-Mediatheque) for the two explicit operations:

```text
publish_to_uckk
import_from_uckk
```

### Sovereign operating environment — kOA-Linux

kOA-Linux is responsible for the conditions under which the ecosystem operates:

- local execution and deployment profiles;
- identity, trust, and isolation;
- verified activation and fail-closed integrity;
- policy enforcement and bounded privilege;
- resource limits and workload admission;
- offline continuity and safe degradation;
- backup, recovery, and rollback;
- audit boundaries and traceable transitions;
- controlled publication, import, and external integration.

It does not absorb the product authority of Konnaxion, Orgo, Kristal Framework, SenTient, SemantiK Architect, or other independently documented systems.

## Social and institutional purpose

kOA treats collective intelligence as infrastructure, not as an automatic wisdom of crowds. A group becomes capable when it can govern the passages between contribution, meaning, knowledge, choice, responsibility, action, and memory.

The design therefore emphasizes:

- democratic legitimacy without technocracy;
- semantic sovereignty and multilingual dignity;
- non-domination, contestability, portability, and exit;
- social cohesion through shared reference points rather than forced uniformity;
- cultural sovereignty through provenance, consent, rights, and community authority;
- durable institutional memory;
- global collaboration combined with local autonomy.
