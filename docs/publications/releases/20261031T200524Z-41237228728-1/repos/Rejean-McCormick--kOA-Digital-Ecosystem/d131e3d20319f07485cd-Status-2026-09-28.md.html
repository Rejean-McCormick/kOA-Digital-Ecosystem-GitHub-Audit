## Domain-neutral projection rule

The ecosystem does not require one universal primary navigation axis. A consumer may be people-first, work-first, place-first, installation/process-first or domain-specific. This is consumer projection configuration, not Kristal/kOA ontology.

Derived glossaries, chronologies and media views may connect referents and assertions but remain rebuildable indexes rather than parallel epistemic canons.
