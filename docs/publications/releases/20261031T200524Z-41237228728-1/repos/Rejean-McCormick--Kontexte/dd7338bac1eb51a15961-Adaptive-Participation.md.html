# Adaptive Participation and Accessible Learning

Kontexte's design premise is **the same public role, with privately adjustable challenges and supports**. A child may appear as the same origami maker or witch as another learner while receiving different steps, tools, expectations, or opportunities for extension.

## Supported design profiles

Mission records include three named design variants:

- `accompagne` — short instructions, pictograms, manipulatives or available assistance.
- `standard` — progressively independent, grade-appropriate work.
- `approfondi` — richer multi-step challenges, information synthesis or justification.

These are **not medical classifications**, official achievement levels, or completed adaptive algorithms. The repository describes the variants; individual assignment and secure delivery to actual learners are **not implemented**.

## Design rules

1. Preserve the learner's choice of outward identity and narrative world; supports should not visibly disclose diagnosis or disability.
2. Offer an accessible entry point without removing all agency or genuine problem-solving.
3. A challenge should be difficult enough to mobilize learning, not so difficult that progress depends on luck or deprivation of crucial information.
4. Allow oral, written, visual and hands-on ways to participate where compatible with the learning target.
5. Provide a safe fallback when another learner is absent or the information exchange fails.
6. Distinguish changing the **means of participation** from changing the **academic learning target**; an educator must review the latter.
7. Record supports actually used when evaluating mastery; do not treat supported task completion as identical to unaided performance.
8. Separate fictional role permissions from physical safety and authenticated school permissions.

## Example: the missing invitation sheet

The basic problem requires six invitations, two invitations per sheet, and only two sheets in stock.

- **Supported path:** arrange six invitation cards in pairs, count the sheets, and physically compare with two available sheets.
- **Standard path:** compute `6 ÷ 2 = 3`, then `3 − 2 = 1` missing sheet.
- **Extended path:** calculate a revised order, consider waste, explain tradeoffs, and communicate a purchase request to another participant.

The same *story* and social identity can be maintained for each path; the learning evidence must specify what each participant actually completed and with which aids.

## Safeguards

Avoid diagnosis labels, automated disability inference, public ability ranking, and rewards that deny essential learning resources. Always provide educator oversight for movement, food, equipment and social contact. Real accessibility quality requires trials with learners and educators; a JSON field alone is not an accommodation.
