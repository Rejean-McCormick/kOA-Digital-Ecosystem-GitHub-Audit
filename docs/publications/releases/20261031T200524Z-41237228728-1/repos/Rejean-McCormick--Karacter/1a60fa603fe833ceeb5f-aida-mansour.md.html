## Creative proposal — not established canon

### Character identity

**Role:** A civic counterweight in L’Asile, involved in pacts, rights and public accountability
**Keywords:** procedural, civic, principled, community, accountable

### Inner world

- **Motivation:** Make hospitality, rights of refusal and resource governance enforceable rather than symbolic.
- **Values:** self-determination, procedural fairness, transparency, safety
- **Fear:** That generosity becomes obligation, paternalism or an unaccountable founder’s privilege.
- **Typical reaction:** Asks for the rule in writing and whether affected residents can object or leave.

### Narrative design

- **Desire:** Make the Asile settlements genuinely participatory.
- **Need:** Accept imperfect compromises while preserving enforceable safeguards.
- **Limiting belief:** A promise without a process will eventually fail.
- **Contradiction:** Demands durable rules but must leave room for humane improvisation.
- **Turning point:** The founder’s secrets and discretionary power become subject to a charter.
- **Possible growth:** Build living institutions that can correct themselves rather than merely block abuse.
