## Source-supported summary

Sun Wukong is challenged to imagine what could be built with the same energy he has used to shatter constraints.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Protect freedom while inventing new possibilities.

**Fear:** Freedom becoming another set of restrictions.

**Narrative contradiction:** A gifted rebel may resist responsibility for what follows.

**Possible growth:** Build something without losing the spirit of rebellion.
