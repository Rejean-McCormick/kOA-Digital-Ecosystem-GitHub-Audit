# Claims and evidence discipline

The Atlas exists to demonstrate possibility without turning possibility into unsupported product claims.

## Derivation

**EXPLICIT** — directly stated by a canonical source.

**COMPOSED** — produced by combining documented capabilities in a way the architecture supports.

**DERIVED** — a reasonable design inference that goes beyond explicit canonical wording.

**FUTURE-DESIGN** — an intentionally proposed direction, not a current claim.

## Runtime evidence

**DOCUMENTED** — behavior or architecture is documented.

**VALIDATED** — supported by direct runtime or implementation evidence.

**PARTIALLY-VALIDATED** — some required pieces are demonstrated, but the full scenario is not.

**UNVERIFIED** — no runtime proof has been established for the complete scenario.

## Public wording rule

Never convert “the architecture could support X” into “Koali does X in production” without the evidence needed to support that stronger statement.

## High-consequence wording

Use explicit boundaries where professional judgment, certified control systems, legal authority, clinical authority, emergency command, or regulated procedures remain authoritative.
