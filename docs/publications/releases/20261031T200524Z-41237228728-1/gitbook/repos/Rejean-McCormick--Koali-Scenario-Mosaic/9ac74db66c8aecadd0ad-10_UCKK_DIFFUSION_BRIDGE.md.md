# Koali ↔ UCKK dissemination bridge

## Role of the bridge

Koali can produce knowledge, decisions, experience, and validated learning objects. UCKK can provide an educational and multimedia channel for turning those objects into forms suited to wider audiences.

A typical bridge may look like:

`Kristal knowledge → Konnaxion collaboration → decision / validation → Orgo production work → UCKK course / media / public channel → audience feedback → renewed knowledge`

## Possible outputs

- course or workshop;
- public explainer;
- video or short-form media;
- podcast or audio guide;
- article or guide;
- infographic;
- media library collection;
- multilingual rendering;
- offline learning package;
- public knowledge channel.

## Critical design principle

Published media should retain a relationship to the knowledge and version it depends on. That makes it possible to flag material as outdated, superseded, contested, or revised rather than letting a media artifact float forever without source context.

## Rebranding / public-good direction

Treat white-label or rebrandable deployment as a **product direction / derived possibility unless and until explicitly canonicalized**. The concept is powerful: an institution could operate the dissemination engine under its own identity while preserving the underlying public-good architecture. Do not present this as an already-proven canonical feature without supporting evidence.
