# Vision and product philosophy

## Product statement

**Karacter is a bilingual, local-first character psychology and design studio.** Users build fictional characters, explore non-judgmental personality preferences, test plausible reactions to situations, compare different perspectives, and create consistent visual representations across settings.

Karacter's **core value is understanding a character, not filling out a long form**. It should work for a parent exploring fictional personalities with a child, as well as a writer, game designer or artist developing coherent fictional casts.

## Three verbs

1. **Create** — identity, visual signature, story, character sheets, image prompts, collections.
2. **Understand** — paired preferences, values, emotional range, contradictions, ties and change.
3. **Simulate** — situational responses, alternative choices, evidence trails and comparisons.

## Foundational principles

1. **No inherently good/bad pole.** Every tendency may help or hinder depending on context.
2. **Preference is not capacity.** Caution 5 / Boldness 1 does not mean a character cannot be courageous.
3. **A score is not a diagnosis.** The axes are creative, not validated psychological instruments.
4. **People and characters contain contradictions.** A fictional axis is an emphasis, not a universal law of behavior.
5. **Reaction suggestions are hypotheses.** The author has final control; a simulated response is not an objective prediction.
6. **Same identity, many appearances.** Theme and image mode never mutate personality/story data.
7. **Local-first and portable.** Users own exports; no account, network dependency or cloud storage is required for core v6.
8. **Simple first, depth when needed.** Explorer and Studio work on the exact same records.
9. **Children are not to be scored or diagnosed.** For learning, discuss fictional examples without labeling real people.
10. **User creative intent outranks automation.** Explicit edits, custom axes and curated portraits must not be silently overridden.

## Positioning

Karacter is **not** a clinical personality inventory, psychotherapy aid, talent assessment, social network for fictional personas, full novel-writing suite, RPG rules engine or image generation provider. It may interoperate with such tools but will not claim their role.

## The differentiator

A traditional character editor asks: *What traits does the character have?* Karacter asks: *What does this character usually prefer? What changes under pressure? What other responses are possible? How might another character interpret the same event?*

## Representative example

**King Klown** is a seemingly playful, cosmic guardian who wants to protect collective freedom but may covertly steer others. His narrative conflict is not "has strong creativity and weak practicality"; it is whether controlling choices to protect freedom undermines the freedom he values. The simulator must be capable of producing both a playful deflection and an honest, direct response depending on context.

## Success criteria

- Authors can build and recover a nuanced character without re-entering information.
- Two contrasting characters produce clearly different **plausible alternatives** in the same scene, with inspectable reasons.
- A character's visual signature remains recognizable across themes and modes.
- Switching FR ↔ EN does not modify creative data, scores or generated files' schema keys.
- A complete backup can be restored, including image bytes, on another browser/device.
