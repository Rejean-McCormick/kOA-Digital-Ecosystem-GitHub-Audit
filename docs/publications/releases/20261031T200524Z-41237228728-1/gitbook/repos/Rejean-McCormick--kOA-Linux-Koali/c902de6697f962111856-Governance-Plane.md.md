# Governance Plane

> **Authority and status:** This wiki is an explanatory navigation layer. The repository contracts, accepted ADRs, normative documents, and executable validators remain authoritative for kOA-Linux Operating System. Public initkoa.org pages explain the wider kOA ecosystem and the product capabilities provided by its independently documented subsystems.

## Purpose

The governance plane turns explicit sociotechnical rules into inspectable decisions about authorization, disclosure, activation, consent, rights, emergency behavior, export and recourse.

## It does not do

A governance result does not directly become root privilege. The system separates civic legitimacy, organizational mandates, knowledge authority, service permissions, signing authority and host operations.

## Main outputs

- authorization or denial;
- disclosure and publication decisions;
- activation decisions;
- policy and version references;
- conditions and expiry;
- receipts and evidence links;
- recourse or review hooks.
