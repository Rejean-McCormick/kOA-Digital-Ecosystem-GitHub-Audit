# Multilingual Knowledge Networks

> **Authority and status:** This wiki is an explanatory navigation layer. The repository contracts, accepted ADRs, normative documents, and executable validators remain authoritative for kOA-Linux Operating System. Public initkoa.org pages explain the wider kOA ecosystem and the product capabilities provided by its independently documented subsystems.

## Intended environment

Several communities or institutions can share structured knowledge while using different languages, terminology and authority channels. The underlying meaning and provenance remain portable; each reader receives a policy-selected rendering appropriate to the available language resources.

## Capability chain

1. Kristal carries structured assertions, provenance, scope and authority.
2. Reader policy selects the permitted view.
3. Semantik Architect renders the selected meaning in a target language.
4. Ariane exposes actions and navigation labels semantically.
5. Konnaxion distributes the resulting knowledge surface.
6. kOA-Linux activates the required packs locally and supports offline use.

## Non-equivalence and disagreement

A term without an exact translation should remain explicitly mapped or unresolved. The system must preserve disputed meanings and authority-scoped language rather than forcing every community into one vocabulary.
