# Ekole Pastorale Wiki

Welcome to the **Ekole Pastorale** technical and editorial wiki.

Ekole Pastorale is the Catholic pastoral education **content repository** loaded by the separate, religion-neutral **Ekole Core**. It holds 32 course Kristals, seven learning pathways, one stand-alone witness Kristal, and a derived Ekole content pack. Its documentation is in English; the current course material remains in Canadian French (`fr-CA`).

> **Prototype notice:** This is a local draft. The curricula, learner views, claims, sources, and third-party rights have **not** been approved for public release or official ecclesiastical teaching.
