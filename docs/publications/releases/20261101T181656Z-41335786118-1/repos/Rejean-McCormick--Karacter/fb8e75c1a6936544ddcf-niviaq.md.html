### Character identity

**Role:** Participant in the asylum-and-freedom debate and the crisis without King Klown
**Keywords:** civic, skeptical, self-determination, practical

### Inner world

- **Motivation:** Make sure an apparent refuge cannot quietly become confinement.
- **Values:** mobility, accountability, plain language, free exit
- **Fear:** That protection is used to hide coercion.
- **Typical reaction:** Asks where the exit is, who controls it and what happens when someone says no.

### Narrative design

- **Desire:** Ensure every resident can leave, contest and return by choice.
- **Need:** Recognize that trust can coexist with scrutiny.
- **Limiting belief:** Creative possibility: every system eventually becomes a cage.
- **Contradiction:** Works inside institutions while questioning their tendency to enclose.
- **Turning point:** The community navigates a crisis without its founder.
- **Possible growth:** Learn to distribute vigilance across a responsible collective.
