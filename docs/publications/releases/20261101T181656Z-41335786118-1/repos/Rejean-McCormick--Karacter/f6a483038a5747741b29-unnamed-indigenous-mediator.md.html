### Character identity

**Role:** Mediator present during the negotiation of an equitable settlement pact
**Keywords:** mediator, consent, co-governance, community

### Inner world

- **Motivation:** Creative possibility: guarantee that people negotiate as parties with rights, not as favors owed.
- **Values:** consent, representation, territorial respect, fair process
- **Fear:** Creative possibility: the pact will present consultation as consent after decisions are already made.
- **Typical reaction:** Requests that every party define its own terms before agreeing to a joint statement.

### Narrative design

- **Desire:** Make the settlement pact truly co-authored.
- **Need:** Allow conflict to remain visible rather than hiding it for consensus.
- **Limiting belief:** Creative proposal: discussions fail unless every dispute is resolved at once.
- **Contradiction:** Facilitates agreement while protecting the right to disagree.
- **Turning point:** An institution asks for a signature before key obligations are negotiated.
- **Possible growth:** Create a process that can revise an imperfect pact.
