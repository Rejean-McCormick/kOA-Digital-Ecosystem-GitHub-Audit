# King Klown Universe

**King Klown** is the unified built-in universe in Karacter 7.4. Its 39 profiles on a clean installation come from two separately documented source-based packs: **Kristal MEDIA** and **King Clown Canon**.

The former `kOA`, Kristal MEDIA, and Canon navigation groups are now a **single universe filter**. The provenance behind each character has *not* been collapsed.

## What is in the catalogue?

The incorporated profiles include King Klown and linked narrative manifestations, characters mentioned in media/story materials, and figures from the narrative canon. Some examples:

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| King Klown continuity | King Klown, Colin Row, other linked manifestations, Réjean McCormick |
| Narrative characters | Aïda Mansour, Aïda Éloïse, Elias, Gabriel Lajoie, other source-mentioned figures |
| Mythic narrative figures | Thot, Loki, Anansi, Amaterasu, Quetzalcoatl, Sun Wukong, among others |

The application also retains source-specific notes. **Not all named figures share one identity.** In particular, King Klown, Colin Row and Réjean McCormick must not be flattened into one interchangeable biography, even where the source describes a continuity between them.

## Canon vs. imaginative enrichment

The supplied records contain both **source-supported observations** and **creative interpretations** added for character design. These must remain distinguishable:

- **Source evidence:** references and descriptions traceable to the supplied canon/media archives.
- **Creative proposals:** personality scales, inferred motivations, aesthetic details, possible growth or fears added to make a playable character.
- **Unresolved questions:** ambiguities that should not be silently resolved as if established.

Where available, open the character's **Source and creative proposals** panel in **Create**. It displays the source summary, creative note and reference paths.

The 39 entries are **not an exhaustive claim** about every person mentioned in all unpublished or long-form manuscripts. Additional characters may be identified later through deeper source analysis.

## Relationships and manifestations

Relationships are links between distinct character records. A manifestation of one continuity should be distinguished from an independent person or an abstract concept. Places, works, publication records and worldbuilding systems are not automatically characters.
