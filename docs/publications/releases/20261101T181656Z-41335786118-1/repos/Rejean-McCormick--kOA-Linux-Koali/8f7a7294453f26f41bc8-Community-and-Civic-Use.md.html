# Community and Civic Use

> **Authority and status:** This wiki is an explanatory navigation layer. The repository contracts, accepted ADRs, normative documents, and executable validators remain authoritative for kOA-Linux Operating System. Public initkoa.org pages explain the wider kOA ecosystem and the product capabilities provided by its independently documented subsystems.

## Intended environment

A community deployment uses Konnaxion for learning, projects, deliberation, collective decisions, distribution and shared memory, while kOA-Linux provides local continuity, trust and separation from private operational data.

## Example flow

1. Local knowledge packages are distributed online or offline.
2. Members learn, discuss and submit proposals.
3. Arguments and evidence remain linked to sources.
4. Decision procedures publish a baseline result and any declared advisory readings.
5. Accepted outcomes become mandates or Orgo cases.
6. Progress and outcomes return to the community under disclosure policy.

## Governance requirements

Participation rules, privacy, consent, authority channels, thresholds, appeals and public evidence must remain explicit. Technical operators cannot silently alter civic legitimacy.
