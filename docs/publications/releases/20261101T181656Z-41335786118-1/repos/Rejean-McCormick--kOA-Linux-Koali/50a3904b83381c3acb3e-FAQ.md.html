# Frequently Asked Questions

> **Reading model:** The main path explains what the kOA ecosystem enables. The technical reference explains how kOA-Linux hosts, isolates, verifies, and operates those capabilities. Product-specific details remain in each subsystem's own documentation.

## Is kOA-Linux the whole kOA ecosystem?

No. kOA-Linux is the sovereign runtime. The wider ecosystem includes independently documented platforms, modules, knowledge systems, language systems, governance services, and cultural layers.

## Where are Konnaxion's modules documented?

In the [Konnaxion Wiki](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Konnaxion/wiki). This Wiki lists the modules only to show how they fit into the knowledge-to-action loop.

## Where is Orgo documented?

In the [Orgo Wiki](https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/Orgo/wiki).

## Is collective intelligence the same as majority rule?

No. The architecture distinguishes equal political standing, advisory credibility, expertise signals, decision procedures, and technical authorization.

## Does kOA require consensus?

No. It aims to make disagreement structured, visible, and actionable while preserving minority positions, uncertainty, and recourse.

## Is UCKK part of kOA-Linux?

No. UCKK is an external Moodle platform. The kOA Mediatheque is the local media authority.

## Can the system work offline?

Yes, for profile-selected capabilities and artifacts present locally. External exchange and remote services are deferred or unavailable explicitly.

## Does AI decide?

No. AI may assist with extraction, summaries, translation, classification, or routing. Authoritative acceptance and sensitive actions remain governed.

## Does a passing documentation validation prove production readiness?

No. It proves the checks implemented for the documentation corpus. Runtime and deployment evidence remain separate.
