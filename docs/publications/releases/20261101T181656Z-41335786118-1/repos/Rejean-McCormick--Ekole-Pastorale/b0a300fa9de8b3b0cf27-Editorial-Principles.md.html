# Editorial Principles

## Catholic identity, without a belief prerequisite for every lesson

Ekole Pastorale is explicitly Catholic. It draws on Scripture, the Church Fathers, the Doctors of the Church, saints and witnesses, the Church's social teaching, worship, prayer, and Christian service.

Its innovation is **pedagogical**, not doctrinal: it presents the message and lived tradition in a separate branch from the miracles and mysteries of faith. Learners can study love, forgiveness, justice, and the life of the saints without first treating miraculous accounts as proofs of the validity of those teachings.

The second branch presents what the Gospels record and what Catholic tradition teaches, including the centrality of Christ's Resurrection. It must not replace Catholic claims with purely symbolic explanations without identifying that reduction as an interpretation outside the intended doctrinal presentation.

## Distinguish source types

| Type | Editorial requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Scriptural passage | Give the biblical reference, literary setting, and the translation and permission used |
| Catholic doctrine | Identify whether a statement is dogmatic teaching, magisterial guidance, theology, or pastoral application |
| Doctor of the Church | Provide author, work, edition, historical setting, and the limited scope of the particular argument |
| Saint or witness | Separate authentic writings, reliable biography, later hagiography, and pastoral interpretation |
| Attributed miracle | Identify the actual source and any documented ecclesial recognition; avoid exaggerating certainty |
| Facilitator's application | Label it as a contemporary pedagogical interpretation rather than an official Church pronouncement |

## Respectful teaching

A course can invite questioning, but the instructor should explain faithfully what Catholicism teaches. Do not require students to publicly disclose private religious beliefs or traumatic experiences. When learners are skeptical, the appropriate response is explanation and respectful discussion, not misrepresentation or pressure.

## Concrete pastoral themes

- Forgiveness alongside justice, personal safety, and boundaries.
- Charity and solidarity without romanticizing involuntary poverty.
- Social teaching and the common good, linked to the contemporary Quebec context.
- Saints' lived virtues, including Saint Francis of Assisi, alongside their written legacy.
- Peace, prophetic conflict, and difficult biblical passages treated honestly.
- Health and healing discussed without displacing professional medical care.

## A single editorial workflow

Draft → check sources and provenance → doctrinal review → safeguarding review → educational review → rights review → competent ecclesial approval when relevant → publish decision → derived Ekole output.
