# Identity, trust and authority

Identity, authentication, trust, authority, admission and ownership are distinct.

The sender transports authority claims; the receiver authenticates, validates, authorizes and admits locally.

IK Admission is not Kristal epistemic validation. IK authority claims are not Kristal Authority Recognition.

Fail closed on incompatible version, invalid schema, unknown trust, unauthorized authority or required artifact-integrity failure.
