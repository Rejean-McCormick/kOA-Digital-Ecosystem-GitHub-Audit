# ARK-16 — Testimony and depositions

The purpose is to preserve a **statement attributed to a person under documented collection circumstances**, without concluding that what the person said is true. Oral history, internal interviews, qualitative research and legal depositions may have very different authorizations and safeguards.

## Core distinctions

- **Collection session:** who collected the account, when and how. The session date is not necessarily the date of alleged events.
- **Declarant:** a role or protected pseudonym; real identity may belong in a separately governed service.
- **Attributed speech:** original answers and statements distinguishable from interviewer questions and reporter paraphrases.
- **Original source:** recording or written media, with provenance and file integrity. Hashes do not establish the truth of its content.
- **Transcript and translation:** derived or separately approved representations, with uncertainty and review status explicit.
- **Correction and version:** preserve revisions, additions, retractions and attestations without silently overwriting the record.
- **Corroboration:** an independently sourced analysis, not part of the witness's words.
- **Privacy:** retention, disclosure and correction procedures belong to the authorized operator.

## Epistemic rule

**"Person X stated P" does not entail "P is true."** Differing accounts may coexist. A summary written by AI is not a signed statement or factual qualification. Consent to participate, consent to record and permission to disclose are separate questions.

## Template limitations

`modeles/deposition-temoignage/design/fiche-deposition-vierge.json` is a **blank, noncanonical design sample** with stable field identifiers. It is not a secure collection app, official deposition procedure, legal advice, or judicial chain-of-custody system. Do not fill distributed templates with real personal data.
