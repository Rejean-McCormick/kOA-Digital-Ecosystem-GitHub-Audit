# Prométhée

**Role:** Gift and responsibility
**Status:** SOURCE_EXPLICIT / MYTHIC
**Source:** `CORPUS/mythos-gods/Prométhée L'étincelle et l'ombre 15709637d3f78096a92cce1065ce67fa.md`

## Source-supported summary

Chained after giving fire to humanity, Prometheus faces what comes after a gift that he can no longer control.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Help others use difficult gifts responsibly.

**Fear:** His gift causing suffering he cannot prevent.

**Narrative contradiction:** Helping humanity cannot mean managing every choice afterward.

**Possible growth:** Accept stewardship through teaching and release, not possession.

**Visual direction:** Rocky cliffs, divine chains and distant embers. — Character visual direction proposed from the source scene, not a fixed canonical design.
