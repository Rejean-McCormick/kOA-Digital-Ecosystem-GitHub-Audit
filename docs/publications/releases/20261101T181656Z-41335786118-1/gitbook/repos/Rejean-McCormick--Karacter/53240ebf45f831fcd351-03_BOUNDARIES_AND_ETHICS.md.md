# Narrative quality, ethical boundaries and product differentiation

## Why Karacter is not a trait checklist

Flat inventories can make writers mistake descriptive adjectives for a dramatic character. Karacter 6 uses four interacting layers: tendencies, values/conflicts, situational facts, and relationships. The engine offers alternatives to spark author judgment, not a mechanical truth function.

## Practical authoring criteria

A well-formed character need not be extremely polarized. Interesting characters may be 3/3 on every axis but have complex motivations. A highly polarized character must still have the option to act contrary to preference when stakes justify it.

## Non-judgmental learning

In comparisons, emphasize that different characters can both have understandable needs. Avoid a single correct personality type, and never score the child or parent using fictional controls. Each reaction can be explored through "What might help?" and "What could be difficult?" rather than "Who is right?".

## Relationship depth

Use specific tensions between characters, not "compatibility" based on scores. Two similarly cautious characters may clash over trust; two different characters may cooperate because of shared values.

## Visual integrity

Visual transformations should serve the same narrative character, not stereotypes about gender, ethnicity, disability or profession. Images are creative depictions, not psychological evidence.

## What not to copy from competitors

Karacter does not need a social feed, 200 mandatory profile fields, RPG combat numbers, or a public character marketplace to accomplish its unique goal. High-value adopted patterns are custom sheets, gallery filtering, bulk workflow review and visible relationship networks; they remain secondary to understanding and simulation.
