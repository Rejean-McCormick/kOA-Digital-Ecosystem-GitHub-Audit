# Tagging and filtering model

The Atlas should be discoverable from several directions without turning the taxonomy into one giant hierarchy.

## Primary category
Exactly one of the eight usage families.

## Pattern family
Exactly one of the 24 recurring pattern families.

## Palette
Multiple human activities may apply to one scenario: find, understand, learn, teach-share, collaborate, create, deliberate, choose, organize, act, respond, coordinate, verify, remember, disseminate.

## Scale
`individual` · `team` · `organization` · `inter-organizational` · `community` · `public-institution`

## Setting
`enterprise` · `research` · `education` · `culture` · `field` · `crisis` · `public-service` · `community`

## Cross-cutting properties
`offline` · `multilingual` · `local-first` · `sensitive-data` · `ai-optional` · `public-facing` · `high-consequence` · `intermittent-connectivity` · `role-routing`

## Components
Kristal · Konnaxion · Orgo · UCKK, with 0–5 editorial intensity.

## Evidence
Always retain `derivation`, `runtime_evidence`, `canon_refs`, and `signature_patterns`. Discovery tags must never erase claim discipline.
