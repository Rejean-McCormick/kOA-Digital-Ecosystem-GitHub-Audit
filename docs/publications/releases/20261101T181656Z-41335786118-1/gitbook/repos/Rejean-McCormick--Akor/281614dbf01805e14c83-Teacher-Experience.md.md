# Teacher Experience

Akor supports the teacher as **designer, facilitator, moderator and pedagogical reviewer**, not as a passive viewer of automated scores.

## Before a mission

A teacher should be able to:

1. Select a **reviewed** mission and its WorldRelease.
2. Inspect the learning objectives and the difference between the original scenario and local adaptations.
3. Review roles, partial information, dependencies, required collaboration and expected artifacts.
4. Choose solo, pair, group or teacher-facilitated delivery where the mission supports it.
5. Review accessibility supports, partner absence fallback and classroom safety constraints.
6. Approve and launch under authenticated permissions; no publication by merely opening a preview.

## During a mission

The target console should display the mission state, groups, role assignments, pending contributions, help requests, checkpoints, disconnections and approved intervention options—**without treating participation count as a grade**.

Suggested interventions: facilitate a hand-off, issue approved help, pause a mission, arrange a substitute, revise group participation where authorized, and stop an unsafe interaction. Each intervention requires a trusted actor and traceable record in the owner system.

## After a mission

A teacher reviews: each learner's actual reasoning and work, supports used, key exchanges and collaboration reflections. Operational success should be reported separately from curricular attainment.

## What exists today

Ekole v4 provides a protected teacher workspace and B1–B8 review assets. The `Ekole_Pont_Missions_v4_1` bundle generates a **local teacher review HTML page** from synthetic sources. It does **not** provide the authenticated console above, nor does it launch live sessions in Orgo.

Akor should integrate with the newer Ekole teacher surface without overwriting its unrelated image editor, Kristal/Spek publication workflow, or local content policies.
