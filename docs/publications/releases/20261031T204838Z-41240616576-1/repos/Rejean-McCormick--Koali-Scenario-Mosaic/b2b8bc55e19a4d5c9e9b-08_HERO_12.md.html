# 12 hero scenarios

These are the first scenarios to show publicly after the v4.3 editorial refresh. They are deliberately memorable, far apart in sector and consequence, and together cover all eight scenario families. Their job is to make one point quickly: **the context can become strange, tense, distributed, political, cultural, or high-consequence, while Koali still preserves continuity between knowledge and action.**

## SCN-003 — A viral video may show a coup — or an almost perfect fabrication

**Pattern:** `PAT-02` Reconstruct context and provenance

**Why it matters:** Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

**Loop:** `sources → provenance → timeline → contradictions → confidence levels → verifiable account`

**Components:** Kristal ●●●●● · Konnaxion ●●●○○ · Orgo ●○○○○ · UCKK ○○○○○
