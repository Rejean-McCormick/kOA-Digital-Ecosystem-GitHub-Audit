# Component lenses

The usage palette describes **what people are trying to accomplish**. Component lenses describe **which parts of the ecosystem contribute most**.

## Kristal — semantic knowledge and memory
Useful when the situation depends on structured knowledge, provenance, source relationships, version history, uncertainty, prior cases, or reusable institutional memory.

**Question:** *What do we know, where did it come from, and how does it relate to this situation?*

## Konnaxion — collective intelligence and collaboration
Useful when people must find one another, learn, contribute, discuss, research, form teams, co-create, or deliberate around shared knowledge objects.

**Question:** *Who should participate, and what can we build or understand together?*

## Orgo — organization and execution
Useful when a signal, request, decision, or plan must become routed work with responsibility, dependencies, evidence, escalation, and closure.

**Question:** *Who does what next, with what context, and how do we know it is complete?*

## UCKK — learning and multimedia dissemination
Useful when knowledge or experience should become a course, guide, media object, public learning resource, or knowledge channel.

**Question:** *How does what we learned become teachable and shareable to the right audience?*

## Secondary lenses

**EkoH / Smart Vote** may support advisory readings, expertise-aware deliberation, or decision context where canonically appropriate; they must not be described as automatically replacing legitimate human authority.

**SemantiK** supports semantic structure and interoperability where documented.

**AI assistance** may summarize, compare, draft, or translate where appropriate, but Koali’s core should not be explained as dependent on an external LLM.

## Reading the scenario scores

Component scores from 0 to 5 are **editorial intensity indicators**, not benchmark measurements. They answer: *how central is this component to explaining the scenario?*
