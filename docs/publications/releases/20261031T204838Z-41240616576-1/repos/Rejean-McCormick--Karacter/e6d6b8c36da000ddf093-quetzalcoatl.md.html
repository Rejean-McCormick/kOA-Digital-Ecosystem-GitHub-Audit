## Source-supported summary

Quetzalcoatl questions a protective instinct that seeks to keep chaos out of a sacred pool, and discovers harmony in movement.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Fear:** Unexpected change destroying a cherished place.

**Narrative contradiction:** His guardianship can become a barrier to growth.

**Possible growth:** Allow the storm to join the dance of life.
