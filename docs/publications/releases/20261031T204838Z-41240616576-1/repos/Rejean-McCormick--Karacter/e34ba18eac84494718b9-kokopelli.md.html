## Source-supported summary

In a still desert, Kokopelli confronts the tension between preserving an inherited melody and allowing new music to grow.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Keep musical memory alive through change.

**Fear:** Losing tradition through experimentation.

**Narrative contradiction:** Perfect repetition can extinguish living tradition.

**Possible growth:** Play a melody that respects memory without imprisoning it.
