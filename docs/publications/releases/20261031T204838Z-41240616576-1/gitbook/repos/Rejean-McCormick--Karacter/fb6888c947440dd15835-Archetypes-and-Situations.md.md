# Archetypes and Situations

## Optional archetype templates

Karacter includes **12 fictional starting templates** available through the character-creation flow. They help you begin with a coherent but editable profile. They are **not** diagnoses, immutable personality classes, or claims about real people.

A template creates a character whose identity and preferences can be modified afterward. If you prefer to build everything yourself, use **New character** or start with John Doe in Blank Universe.

Typical creative approaches include an explorer, observer, protector, dreamer, mediator, trickster, inventor, or other archetypal roles. The exact template names are available in the application's localized picker.

## Scenarios are editable

The **Simulate** screen contains built-in everyday scenarios and supports **new custom situations**. A situation includes a title, context, guiding question, tags, and default stakes. You can edit its contents. The application allows deletion of user-created scenarios; built-in ones are protected from deletion in the UI.

## Three levels of stakes

| Stakes | Example narrative implication |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Mild inconvenience or playful disagreement |
| **Medium** | A meaningful personal or social consequence |
| **High** | Serious loss, loyalty, danger, or urgent decision |

Stakes shape the simulation's context but do not make any personality type automatically good or bad.

## The four essential questions

When you examine a candidate reaction, ask:

1. **Feeling:** What emotions might coexist?
2. **Interpretation:** What story is the character telling themselves?
3. **Action:** What might they actually do?
4. **Need and trade-off:** What are they trying to protect, and at what cost?

Karacter produces three options with evidence so you can compare alternatives, revise the wording, or reject them all.
