# Dionysos

**Role:** Joy and transformation
**Status:** SOURCE_EXPLICIT / MYTHIC
**Source:** `CORPUS/mythos-gods/Dionysos Le calice débordant 15709637d3f780dfbecdfdf408b23e6e.md`

## Source-supported summary

Dionysos confronts the difference between inspiring joy and being consumed by endless giving; the cup becomes a figure of renewal.

## Creative proposals — not canon

**Motivation:** Transform sorrow through shared celebration.

**Fear:** Joy turning into an obligation without rest.

**Narrative contradiction:** His generosity can overflow without limits.

**Possible growth:** Find rhythms of renewal as well as abundance.
