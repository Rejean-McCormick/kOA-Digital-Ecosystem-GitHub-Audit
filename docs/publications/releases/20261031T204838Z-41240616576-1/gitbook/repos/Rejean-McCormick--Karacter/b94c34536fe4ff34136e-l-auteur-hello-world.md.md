# L’Auteur (Hello World)

> **Kind:** meta-fictional-character · **Source status:** CANON / CANON_SOURCE_CURRENT

## Source-supported facts

This character exists specifically within Hello World and is not a general alias for King Klown or the real-world creator.

**Traceable references:** `claim:C073`, `claim:C074`, `claim:C075`, `claim:C083`, `U231`

## Creative proposal — not established canon

Never use a private civil name or turn the metafictional character into a factual biographical profile.

### Character identity

**Role:** A figure at the boundary of authorship and the fictional autopsy of King Klown
**Keywords:** meta-fiction, origin, creator-character, recursive, boundary

### Inner world

- **Motivation:** Face the questions of who creates whom and where an invented figure begins or ends.
- **Values:** responsibility, truth in fiction, boundaries, memory
- **Fear:** That the distinction between creator, narrator and fictional being collapses.
- **Typical reaction:** Stops the explanation at the authorship boundary and asks whether the witness is part of the story.

### Narrative design

- **Desire:** Understand the recursive origin of the King Klown figure.
- **Need:** Allow some questions to remain framed rather than answered as biography.
- **Limiting belief:** Every layer must have a single original author.
- **Contradiction:** Exists as a character through the very work that traces its own provenance.
- **Turning point:** The final investigation finds a reader rather than a single final explanation.
- **Possible growth:** Live with layered authorship without claiming to be the real-world creator.

### Visual direction — prompt suggestion, no source portrait

An intentionally non-identifying silhouette seated beside a desk and unfinished pages; atmospheric ink and white light, with the face out of view.

**Anchors:** unfinished manuscript, desk lamp, indistinct silhouette

**Exclusions:** real-person likeness, civil name, claimed documentary portrait
