# Frequently Asked Questions

### Where are the characters? I only see King Klown and Mira.

Select **All universes** in the sidebar. A **fresh Karacter 7.4 installation** includes 39 King Klown profiles plus John Doe in Blank Universe. If you are opening an old version, update to 7.4. In 7.4, the built-in packs are installed automatically; you should **not** have to import them manually. See [Universe Navigation](Universe-Navigation).

### Why are there only two built-in universes?

That is the current product decision: **King Klown** and **Blank Universe**. The old kOA / MEDIA / Canon *filters* are consolidated under King Klown, while the original source information remains within the profiles. You can create additional universes yourself.

### Is “All universes” a third universe?

No. It is a multi-universe **view filter**.

### Why is John Doe almost empty?

He is deliberately a blank starting profile with no psychological traits imposed. See [Blank Universe](Blank-Universe).

### Does a score of 6 mean that a trait is good?

No. Scores represent **relative habitual preference on a creative axis**. Both poles have value and possible trade-offs.

### Can a person be cautious and audacious?

Yes. Human traits are not strictly mutually exclusive. The two linked sliders represent a **contextual storytelling preference**, not a scientific constraint on a real person.

### Does a simulation predict behavior?

No. It produces **three narrative hypotheses**, each with an explanation. The author may edit, reject or canonize one.

### Will running a simulation change a character's story?

No. Only **Make canon** creates an approved story event. Applying growth to a belief is a separate explicit action.

### Does switching a visual theme change personality?

No. A visual theme changes the illustration brief and image slot, not core personality or story history.

### Does Karacter generate images?

It produces prompts. The images are made with an **external generator** and then imported into Karacter.

### Can I save a character in TXT?

Yes, but TXT omits image bytes and the shared canonical timeline. Use **Backup ZIP** for a complete recovery archive.

### Why is the backup labeled v6 when Karacter is 7.4?

**v6 is the portable backup schema**, maintained for compatibility. The app and documentation are version **7.4**.

### Does the FR/EN switch translate my own writing?

No. It changes supported UI strings and some built-in responses, not authored descriptions and names.

### Can I share a library between computers?

You can export a full JSON/ZIP and import it elsewhere. **Automatic synchronization** is not included.

### Why do source-aware King Klown characters contain imaginative details?

Some psychological preferences and design cues were added as **creative proposals**. The character's source panel distinguishes source-supported details from interpretations. See [King Klown Universe](King-Klown-Universe).
