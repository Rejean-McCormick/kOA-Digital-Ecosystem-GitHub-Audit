# Kristal, Da’at and the knowledge boundary

Kristal is the ecosystem's **structured epistemic/reference system**. Da’at is the anti-corruption/mapping boundary used to enter Kristal-native contracts.

## Current authority flow

```text
source/provider discovery
        ↓
EncyKlopedia
        ↓
Da’at
        ↓
Kristal
        ↓
UCKK / Univers-Cité and other consumers
```

## Kristal lifecycle

```text
Structured Epistemic State
    ↓ compile
Working Exchange
    ↓ review / validation / authority recognition
Reference Exchange
    ↓ policy-dependent derivation
Runtime Pack / consumer projection
```

Claim-IR and Resolved Claim-IR may exist in extraction/resolution profiles, but they are not mandatory universal stages.

## Referent identity

The current baseline uses **Referent Registry `1.0.0`** as a domain-neutral identity layer. External identifiers may support matching/provenance; they do not replace Kristal-owned referent semantics or assertion status.

## EncyKlopedia responsibilities

EncyKlopedia owns source discovery/acquisition, provider resolution, immutable evidence/provenance, source snapshots and referent candidates. It does not validate epistemic truth merely by acquiring material.

## Da’at responsibilities

Da’at maps source/IK-facing payloads into Kristal-native contracts, enforces the declared mapping/ACL boundary and preserves provenance. It does not become owner of source operational state or Kristal authority semantics.

## Kristal responsibilities

Kristal owns referent semantics, assertions, evidence/provenance, certainty, validation, authority recognition, Reader Policy and related epistemic artifacts.

## UCKK / Univers-Cité responsibilities

UCKK owns scoped consumer projection choices such as primary view, Moodle materialization, derived glossary, media presentation and local learning/campus state. A projection is rebuildable from a pinned Kristal state and does not become the Kristal source of truth.

See [Knowledge acquisition and projection](Knowledge-Projection).
