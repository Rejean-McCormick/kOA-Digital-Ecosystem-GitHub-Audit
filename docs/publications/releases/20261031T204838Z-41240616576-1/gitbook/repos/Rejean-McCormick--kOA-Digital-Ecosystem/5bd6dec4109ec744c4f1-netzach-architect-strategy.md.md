# Netzach — Architect Strategy (conceptual role)

Architect-Strategy turns **Reader-Policy-eligible knowledge plus operating context** into governed work proposals.

Inputs may reference Working or Reference material when the active Reader Policy/Profile permits. Strategy must preserve epistemic labels and must not create validation, valuation or authority recognition by presentation.

Outputs are proposals/plans for an owning operational system (for example Orgo), not direct mutations of Konnaxion, Kristal or kOA-Linux state.
